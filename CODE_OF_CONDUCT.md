# Community Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to act with respect, assume good intent while
addressing impact, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or
publication of private information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. The owner has
not supplied a private contact address yet; configure one before enabling
public contributions. Do not use public issue reports for sensitive concerns.

The maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where
possible, and take action proportionate to the circumstances.
